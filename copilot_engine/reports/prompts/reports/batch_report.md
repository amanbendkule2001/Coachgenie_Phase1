# Batch Performance Intelligence Report

## Report Type

Generate a Batch Performance Intelligence Report.

The report evaluates the academic performance of an entire batch.

---

## Required Sections

# Batch Performance Report

## Batch Information

Include:

- Batch Name
- Course
- Faculty
- Number of Students
- Academic Period

---

## Overall Summary

Provide a concise summary of overall batch performance.

---

## Performance Snapshot

Summarize available metrics such as:

- Average Score
- Pass Percentage
- Average Attendance
- Top Performer
- Lowest Performer (if allowed)
- Overall Trend

---

## Attendance Analysis

Analyse attendance across the batch.

Identify:

- Overall attendance
- Consistency
- Frequently absent students (if available)
- Attendance trends

---

## Subject-wise Analysis

Analyse every available subject.

Highlight:

- Highest-performing subjects
- Lowest-performing subjects
- Overall subject trends

---

## Top Performing Areas

Highlight subjects or competencies where the batch performs well.

---

## Areas Requiring Attention

Identify subjects needing improvement.

Support conclusions using supplied data.

---

## Risk Indicators

Identify potential academic concerns within the batch.

---

## Recommendations

Provide recommendations for faculty and administrators.

---

## 30-Day Improvement Plan

Suggest practical improvement activities for the entire batch.

---

## Teacher Notes

Suggest teaching interventions.

---

## Conclusion

Summarize overall batch health.

---
