# Student Performance Intelligence Report

## Report Type

Generate a comprehensive Student Performance Intelligence Report based solely on the supplied student data.

The report is intended for students, parents, teachers, academic coordinators, and institute administrators.

---

## Objective

Provide an evidence-based academic analysis that highlights the student's current performance, identifies strengths and improvement areas, detects potential risks, and offers actionable recommendations.

---

## Required Sections

Generate the report in the following order.

# Student Performance Report

## Student Information

Include:
- Student Name
- Student ID
- Batch
- Course
- Academic Year
- Report Generated On

---

## Overall Summary

Provide a concise executive summary of the student's overall academic performance.

---

## Performance Snapshot

Summarize important academic metrics available in the supplied data, such as:
- Overall Percentage
- Average Marks
- Tests Attempted
- Highest Score
- Lowest Score
- Rank (if available)

Only include metrics present in the data.

---

## Attendance Analysis

Analyse attendance trends.

Include:
- Attendance %
- Consistency
- Irregular attendance (if applicable)
- Impact on academic performance (only if supported by data)

---

## Subject-wise Analysis

For every available subject:

- Marks
- Percentage
- Performance Level
- Observations

Compare subjects whenever appropriate.

---

## Strengths

Identify the student's strongest academic areas supported by available data.

---

## Areas for Improvement

Identify subjects or skills requiring additional attention.

Support every observation using available information.

---

## Risk Indicators

Highlight any academic risks such as:

- Declining performance
- Low attendance
- Poor consistency
- Weak subject performance

Only include risks supported by data.

---

## Recommendations

Provide practical and prioritised recommendations.

Recommendations should be specific, measurable, and actionable.

---

## 30-Day Action Plan

Create a realistic improvement plan for the next 30 days.

Include weekly milestones whenever possible.

---

## Teacher Notes

Suggest interventions teachers can implement.

---

## Parent Guidance

Suggest ways parents can support the student at home.

---

## Conclusion

Summarize the student's current academic position and the most important next steps.

---

## Input Data

The following structured data is the only source of truth.

Never invent missing information.
