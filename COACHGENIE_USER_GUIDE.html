1. Getting Started & Logging In Authentication

CoachGenie is a multi-tenant platform where each coaching institute has its own isolated subdomain environment.

  • Step 1: Open your web browser and navigate to http://localhost:3000/login.
  • Step 2: Enter your institute's Institute Subdomain (e.g. demo).
  • Step 3: Enter your assigned email address and password, then click Sign In.
  • Step 4: Upon authentication, you will be redirected to your role-specific dashboard.
2. Institute Owner / Admin Guide Full Control
🏢 Managing Institute Dashboard & Finances Owner Role
  • Executive Dashboard (/dashboard): View real-time revenue collection, total active students, pending fee dues, and lead conversion rates.
  • Staff Management (/settings/users): Create and manage accounts for Sales Counselors, Tutors, Students, and Parents. Assign role permissions.
  • Fee Structure & Invoicing (/fees):
    • Create fee templates for courses/batches (e.g. Class 10 Physics Annual Fee: ₹25,000).
    • Generate invoices for admitted students with due dates and custom discounts.
    • Record offline Cash/UPI payments and view monthly revenue trends.
  • Admissions Approval (/admissions): Review incoming student admission requests, verify submitted documents, approve admissions, and automatically generate student enrollment records.
3. Sales Counselor Guide (Leads & Admissions) Sales Pipeline
🎯 Managing Sales Leads & Conversions Counselor Role
  • Leads Kanban Board (/leads): Track prospective students across all 8 pipeline stages:
    New → Contacted → Interested → Demo Scheduled → Demo Done → Negotiation → Enrolled → Lost.
  • Creating a New Lead: Click + Add Lead, enter student name, phone number, target exam, course interest, and lead source (e.g. Website, Instagram, Referral).
  • Scheduling Follow-ups: Click on any lead card to record activity notes, set follow-up reminder dates, and log call outcomes.
  • Converting Lead to Admission: When a lead agrees to join, click Convert Lead to auto-fill their information directly into the Admission Form.
4. Tutor & Educator Guide (Classes, Attendance & Exams) Academic Management
📚 Managing Batches, Classes & Marks Tutor Role
  • Daily Class Scheduling (/batches): Schedule live or offline classes for your assigned batches, select subject topics, and set start/end times.
  • Syllabus Progress Tracking (/syllabus): Mark syllabus topics as Not Started, In Progress, or Completed. CoachGenie automatically calculates overall batch syllabus completion %.
  • Daily Student Attendance (/attendance): Select batch date and mark students as Present, Absent, or Late. Low attendance warnings trigger automatically.
  • Exams & Student Marks (/exams): Create unit tests/mock exams, specify maximum marks, enter student scores, and generate class performance rankings.
  • Student Growth Cards (/growth-cards): Evaluate student soft skills, discipline, class participation ratings, and provide teacher remarks.
5. Student & Parent Portal Guide Learning & Transparency
🎓 Student & Parent Self-Service Features Student / Parent Role
  • My Classes & Schedule: View upcoming batch classes, assigned subjects, and tutor details.
  • Syllabus Tracker: View real-time subject syllabus completion progress.
  • Attendance & Exam Report Cards: Check personal monthly attendance percentage and exam score breakdowns.
  • Fee Receipts & Statements: Parents can view total fees, paid amounts, remaining balance, and historical payment receipts.
  • AI Copilot Assistant (/ai): Students can ask questions to the AI Copilot to get instant learning assistance, study guidance, and practice insights.
