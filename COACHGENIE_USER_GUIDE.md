## 🚀 1. Getting Started & Logging In

1. Open your web browser and navigate to `http://localhost:3000/login`.
2. Enter your coaching institute's **Subdomain** (e.g. `demo`).
3. Enter your assigned email address and password, then click **Sign In**.
4. Upon authentication, you will be automatically redirected to your role-specific dashboard.

---

## 🏢 2. Institute Owner / Admin Guide

### 📊 Executive Dashboard (`/dashboard`)
* View real-time revenue collection, total enrolled students, pending fee dues, and lead conversion rates.

### 👥 Staff Management (`/settings/users`)
* Create and manage accounts for Sales Counselors, Tutors, Students, and Parents. Assign custom role permissions.

### 💰 Fee Structure & Invoicing (`/fees`)
* **Create Fee Templates**: Define course fee structures (e.g. *Class 10 Annual Fee: ₹25,000*).
* **Generate Invoices**: Issue invoices to students with due dates and custom discounts.
* **Payment Settlement**: Record Cash/UPI payments. Invoices automatically update to `PAID` state once fully settled.

### 📝 Admissions Approval (`/admissions`)
* Review incoming student admission forms, verify submitted documents, approve applications, and auto-generate student enrollment records.

---

## 🎯 3. Sales Counselor Guide (Leads & Admissions)

### 📊 Sales Pipeline Kanban (`/leads`)
Track prospective students across all 8 pipeline stages:
`New` ➔ `Contacted` ➔ `Interested` ➔ `Demo Scheduled` ➔ `Demo Done` ➔ `Negotiation` ➔ `Enrolled` ➔ `Lost`

### ➕ Creating & Managing Leads
1. Click **+ Add Lead**, enter student name, phone number, target exam, and lead source (e.g. *Website, Referral*).
2. Click any lead card to schedule follow-up dates, set reminder notes, and log activity outcomes.
3. Click **Convert Lead** when a student enrolls to auto-populate their data directly into the Admission Form.

---

## 📚 4. Tutor & Educator Guide (Classes, Attendance & Exams)

### 📅 Batch & Class Scheduling (`/batches`)
* Schedule live or offline classes for assigned batches, select subject topics, and set start/end times.

### 📖 Syllabus Progress Tracking (`/syllabus`)
* Update syllabus topics as `Not Started`, `In Progress`, or `Completed`. CoachGenie automatically calculates overall batch syllabus completion %.

### 📋 Daily Student Attendance (`/attendance`)
* Select batch date and mark students as `Present`, `Absent`, or `Late`. Low attendance warnings trigger automatically.

### 🏆 Exams & Student Marks (`/exams`)
* Create unit tests/mock exams, specify maximum marks, enter student scores, and generate class performance rankings.

### 🌟 Student Growth Cards (`/growth-cards`)
* Evaluate student soft skills, discipline, class participation ratings, and provide teacher remarks.

---

## 🎓 5. Student & Parent Portal Guide

* **My Classes & Schedule**: View upcoming batch classes, assigned subjects, and tutor details.
* **Syllabus Tracker**: View real-time subject syllabus completion progress.
* **Attendance & Exam Report Cards**: Check personal monthly attendance percentage and exam score breakdowns.
* **Fee Receipts & Statements**: Parents can view total fees, paid amounts, remaining balance, and historical payment receipts.
* **AI Copilot Assistant (`/ai`)**: Students can ask questions to the AI Copilot to get instant learning assistance, study guidance, and practice insights.
